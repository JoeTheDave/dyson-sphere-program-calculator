# Dyson Sphere Program Calculator — Vision

## Purpose
A personal production chain calculator for Dyson Sphere Program. Given a
target item, it computes the full ingredient tree, required machine counts,
and throughput rates — replacing manual math when planning factory builds.

## Intended Use
Built for personal use. Publicly accessible at the Fly.io URL but not
advertised. Others are welcome to use it if they find the link.

## Business Model
None. Free, open-source personal project.

## Roadmap
Essentially complete. Ongoing work limited to bug fixes and minor improvements
as they surface during play.

## Out of Scope
No specific constraints. Open to improvements on a case-by-case basis.
